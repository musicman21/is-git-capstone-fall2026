# Recipe Name: Banana Pudding

**Contributed by:**
Mason Kuenneke

## Ingredients
- 3/4 cup of sugar
- 1/4 cup of all-purpose flour
- 1/4 teaspoon of salt
- 3 cups of chilled 2% milk
- 3 large eggs
- 1 and 1/2 teaspoons of vanilla extract
- 8 ounces of vanilla wafers (or about 60 cookies)
- 4 large ripe bananas, cut into 1/4-inch slices

## Steps
Instructions:
1. In a large saucepan, mix the sugar, flour, and the salt. Whisk in the milk, then cook and stir over medium heat until thickened and bubbly.
2. Reduce the heat to low; cook and stir for 2 minutes longer. Remove from heat.
3. In a small bowl, whisk the eggs. Whisk a small amount of the hot mixture into the eggs; return all to pan, whisking constantly. 
4. Bring to a gentle boil; cook and stir for 2 minutes. Remove from heat. Stir in vanilla. Cool 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
5. In an ungreased 8-in square baking dish, layer 25 vanilla wafers, half the banana slices, and half the pudding. Repeat layers.
6. Press plastic wrap onto the surface of the pudding. Refrigerate 4 hours or overnight. 
7. Just before serving, remove the plastic wrap; crush the remaining wafers and sprinkle them over the top.
8. Enjoy!
