# \# Chocolate Chip Cookies

# 

# \*\*Contributed by:\*\* Kandice Johnson

# 

# \## Ingredients

# \- 1 cup butter, softened

# \- 1 cup white sugar

# \- 1 cup brown sugar

# \- 2 eggs

# \- 2 teaspoons vanilla extract

# \- 3 cups all-purpose flour

# \- 1 teaspoon baking soda

# \- 1/2 teaspoon salt

# \- 2 cups chocolate chips

# 

# \## Steps

# 1\. Preheat the oven to 350°F.

# 2\. Mix the softened butter, white sugar, and brown sugar together.

# 3\. Add the eggs and vanilla and mix well.

# 4\. In a separate bowl, combine the flour, baking soda, and salt.

# 5\. Slowly mix the dry ingredients into the wet ingredients.

# 6\. Stir in the chocolate chips.

# 7\. Place spoonfuls of cookie dough onto a baking sheet.

# 8\. Bake for 10 to 12 minutes or until the edges are lightly golden.

# 9\. Let the cookies cool before serving.

\## Baking Tip

For softer cookies, remove them from the oven when the edges are golden but the centers still look slightly soft.
