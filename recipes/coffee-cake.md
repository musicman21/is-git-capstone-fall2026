\# Coffee Cake with Heath Toffee Topping

 

\*\*Contributed by:\*\* Alexandra Yeager

 

\## Ingredients

\- 2 cups all-purpose flour

\- 1 cup granulated sugar

\- 1 teaspoon baking powder

\- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda

\- 1/2 teaspoon salt

\- 1/2 cup butter, softened

\- 2 large eggs

\- 1 cup milk

\- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

 

\### Topping

\- 1 cup Heath toffee bits

\- 1/2 cup brown sugar

\- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon

\- 1/4 cup melted butter

 

\## Steps

1\. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).

2\. Grease a 9-inch baking pan.

3\. In a large bowl, mix the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.

4\. Add the softened butter, eggs, milk, and vanilla extract and mix until combined.

5\. Pour the batter into the prepared baking pan.

6\. In a small bowl, mix the Heath toffee bits, brown sugar, cinnamon, and melted butter.

7\. Sprinkle the topping evenly over the batter.

8\. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

9\. Allow the cake to cool for 15 minutes before serving.

 

\## Notes

\- Best served warm with coffee.

\- Store leftovers in an airtight container for up to 3 days.

\- Sprinkle extra Heath toffee bits on top after baking for added crunch.

\## Baking Tips

\- Let the cake cool for 10 to 15 minutes before slicing to help it hold its shape.
