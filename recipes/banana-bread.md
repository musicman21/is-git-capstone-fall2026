# Banana Bread

**Contributed by: Auvion Cook**

## Ingredients

* &#x20;flour, sugar, baking soda, butter, eggs, and ripe bananas

## Steps

11. **Preheat & Prep:** Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C) and lightly grease a 4x8 inch loaf pan.
12. **Mash & Mix:** In a large mixing bowl, mash the ripe bananas with a fork until smooth. Melt your butter in the microwave (using 10-15 second bursts) or in a small saucepan over low heat on the stovetop. Let it cool slightly, then stir the melted butter directly into the mashed bananas.
13. **Combine:** Lightly beat the eggs in a separate bowl with a fork or a hand whisk until the yolks and whites are just blended (avoid over-mixing with an electric mixer so it doesn't foam up). Mix the beaten eggs, sugar, baking soda, salt, and vanilla extract into your banana mixture. Whisk thoroughly.
14. **Add Flour:** Gently fold in the all-purpose flour until the batter is just combined. Do not overmix.
15. **Bake:** Pour the batter into your prepared loaf pan. Bake for 50 to 60 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
16. **Cool:** Remove from the oven and let cool in the pan for 10 minutes before transferring to a wire rack.
