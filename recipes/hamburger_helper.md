# Hamburger Helper Recipe

**Contributed by: Evi Naumann**

## Ingredients
-1 pound ground beef
-1/2 cup of water
-2 1/2 cups of milk
-Seasoning packet
-Noodles

## Cookware
-Medium sized skillet
-A utensil to mix the ingredients (Such as a spatula or large spoon.)

## Steps
1. Put your ground beef into the skillet and cook for 5-10 minutes until browned.
2. Drain the grease from the skillet. (Make sure to not drain in a sink)
3. Mix the water, milk, seasoning, and noodles in the skillet. 
4. Cook on medium heat for 10 minutes until the sauce thickens and the noodles are fully cooked.
5. Your hamburger helper is ready for eating.
