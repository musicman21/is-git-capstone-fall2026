# Chocolate Pie Recipe

**Contributed by: Jianbin Dong**

## Ingredients

- 1 baked 9-inch pie crust
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- ¼ cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- ¼ cup cornstarch or flour
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 3 cups whole milk
- 3 large egg yolks

## Flavoring

- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 2 tablespoons unsalted butter

## Steps
1. Prepare and bake a 9-inch pie crust if using a homemade crust. Let it cool completely.
2. Combine the sugar, cocoa powder, cornstarch or flour, and salt in a saucepan.
3. Gradually whisk in the whole milk until the mixture is smooth.
4. Add the egg yolks and cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the filling thickens.
5. Remove the saucepan from the heat and stir in the vanilla extract and butter.
6. Pour the chocolate filling into the cooled pie crust.
7. Refrigerate the pie until the filling is fully set before serving.
